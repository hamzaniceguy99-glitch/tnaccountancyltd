Program 03 · Sixty days, not next January

Capital gains on disposals

A residential disposal must be reported and paid within sixty days of completion, separately from the annual return. Tell us when a sale is agreed rather than when it completes, so the computation is ready and reliefs like private residence relief are claimed properly instead of retrospectively.

Program 04 · Often the answer is no

Structure reviews

Incorporating a portfolio is widely promoted and frequently wrong: the stamp duty on transfer, the capital gains on disposal to the company and the mortgage terms often outweigh the tax saved. We run the numbers for your actual position, and in many cases the honest answer is to leave it alone.
